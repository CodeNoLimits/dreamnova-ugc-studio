# Adapt the method to any business (build a profile in 5 minutes)

If they give a website: read it first (and optionally `creative_create_brand_kit_from_website`), fill what you can,
then ask only for the gaps — one question at a time. Save the result as `profiles/<business>.md` so the next
video starts instantly.

```markdown
# Profile — <Business name>
- Person on camera: <name, role> · languages: <he/en/fr> · currency per language: HE=₪ EN=$ FR=€ (never mixed)
- What they sell: <offer, price range if public>
- Who buys: <audience in one sentence — who, their problem, what they want instead>
- The ONE thing they want viewers to believe: <core message>
- Their own key phrases (verbatim): <3–6 sentences they really say>
- Proof they actually have: <results, years, public facts — nothing invented>
- Call to action: <one action + where it leads>
- Tone: <e.g. calm premium expert / funny friend / energetic coach> · words to use / words to avoid
- Look: colours (hex), font, logo, backgrounds they like / dislike
- Voice: ElevenLabs voice_id <…> (cloned with consent) · fallback voice
- Platforms + formats: <Reels 9:16 30 s, YouTube 16:9 …>
- Never: <topics, claims, competitors, people, words>
```

Quick interview order (stop as soon as you have enough): 1) what do you sell and to whom, 2) what result do
clients get (true examples), 3) what should a stranger feel after 30 seconds, 4) where will it be posted,
5) can you film yourself, or should we use an AI version of you / faceless.
