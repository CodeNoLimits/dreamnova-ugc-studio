# The DreamNova UGC technique

UGC = video that looks and sounds like a real person talking to one viewer on their phone, not like an ad.
It wins because it is fast, specific and human. Everything below serves that.

## 1. The hook (first 1–2 seconds decide everything)

Write 6, pick 1. Each hook is said out loud AND shown as the first caption. Six proven shapes:

| Shape | Example (expert on YouTube) |
|---|---|
| Contrarian truth | "Instagram is rented land." |
| Costly mistake | "The post you published today dies in 24 hours." |
| Named rule / number the person really uses | "There's a 7-hour rule before anyone trusts you." |
| Direct question to the right viewer | "Running a service business above 20K a month?" |
| Before → after tension | "From chasing clients to clients asking for you." |
| Pattern break (visual) | Start mid-sentence, or hold up the object, or walk into frame |

Rules: no greeting, no "hi I'm…", no logo intro. Hook = the most surprising TRUE sentence of the script.
The first frame must already show a face or the product, never a title card.

## 2. Formats (pick one per video)

| Format | Shape | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| **Authority talking head** | one person, straight to camera, hook → idea → example → CTA, 20–45 s | experts, consultants, agencies |
| **Yapper story** | personal story whose turn is the product, 30–60 s | founders, coaches |
| **Reaction + demo** | 2–3 s reaction with a hook caption, HARD cut to the product/screen doing the thing | apps, tools, products |
| **No-yapping tutorial** | silent, music only, step captions, split screen input/result | how-tos, software |
| **Greenscreen commentary** | person cut out over a post, chart or screenshot they react to | news, myths, competitor takes |
| **Myth vs truth** | "Everyone says X. Here's what actually happens." 3 beats | education |
| **Faceless b-roll + voice** | AI or stock b-roll, voiceover, kinetic captions | when the person cannot film |
| **Mini-VSL** | problem → why it happens → solution → proof → offer, 60–180 s | high-ticket services |
| **Launch video (/brag)** | site/app UI animated, 15–25 s | websites, apps, new features |

Build a small **portfolio** (3 formats × 2 hooks) instead of betting on one video.

## 3. Script skeleton (≈0.4 s per spoken word)

```
HOOK      1 sentence, ≤ 9 words
TENSION   why the viewer should care now (1–2 sentences)
IDEA      the one insight, said simply
EXAMPLE   one concrete picture (a client situation, a number they really have, a before/after)
PROOF     only TRUE proof: their own result, a public fact, a demo on screen
CTA       one action ("comment GUIDE", "link in bio", "book a call") — never two
```
Short sentences, spoken language, contractions. Cut every word that does not move the viewer.
If the person already said it well in their own words, use their words verbatim.

## 4. Performance (real person or AI avatar)

- Fast, natural, conversational delivery; small smiles; eyebrows and hands that follow the meaning.
  A frozen face or a flat voice kills UGC.
- Eye line = the lens. Phone at eye level, vertical, window light in front, quiet room, lavalier if possible.
- For ElevenLabs voices: `eleven_v3` with audio tags where the delivery changes (`[warmly]`, `[excited]`,
  `[laughs softly]`, `[pause]`), punctuation for rhythm, CAPITALS sparingly for emphasis.
- AI avatars: one emotion per take, aligned across photo + voice + prompt (energetic voice + neutral photo
  + calm prompt = uncanny).

## 5. Editing grammar

- Cut on intention every 1.5–3 s: change of shot size (wide → medium → close), a b-roll insert, or a
  screen/product shot. A digital zoom alone is not a new shot.
- Remove breaths before sentences, "umm", false starts and any silence > 0.4 s.
- Captions: always on (most people watch muted), 2–4 words per line, inside the safe zone
  (vertical: nothing important in the top 220 px, bottom 500 px, or 180 px on each side).
- Sound: voice at 100 %, music ducked well under it (`ugc.py render --music`), final loudness −16 LUFS.
  For organic TikTok/Reels you may post without music and add a trending sound in the app.
- End on the CTA line or loop back to the first image so replays feel seamless.

## 6. Variations matrix (how one idea becomes 10 videos)

`hooks (3) × formats (2) × length (15 s / 40 s)` → 12 cuts from one recording session.
Test, keep the winner's hook style, retire the losers. Volume of honest tests is the strategy.

## 7. What never ships

Invented testimonials or numbers · an AI actor presented as a real client · a real person's face that is not
really theirs · text generated by the video model · captions that differ from what is said · cropped heads
or hands cut at the frame edge · music louder than speech · a watermark or another brand's logo in frame.
